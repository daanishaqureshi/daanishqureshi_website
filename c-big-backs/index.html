Free Food · Departures

Saw food? Add it

Anyone with a university address can put an event on the board.

  1. Email with FREE FOOD in the subject.
  2. Paste the announcement text, or attach a photo of the poster.
  3. It gets screened and appears here, credited to your first name and last initial.
Email a new event

Only .edu senders are accepted. Anything else is dropped without a reply.

How this works

Three sources feed the board: the public Yale events calendar, department announcement emails, and events people email in themselves.

Every listing is screened by a Claude model before it reaches the board. The model decides whether the food is real and free, and its verdict, confidence, and the line of source text it leaned on are shown under each event, so you can judge the call yourself.
